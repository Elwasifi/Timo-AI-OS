TEMO AI OS Motion & Animation SpecificationThis document defines the complete motion language, timing, physics, camera movement, and micro-interactions for TEMO AI OS. It bridges the visual design bible, component library, and page layouts into a unified kinetic system.  1. Motion PrinciplesThe motion architecture of TEMO AI OS adheres to four core principles:Executive Precision: UI transitions are swift, crisp, and predictable ($150\text{ ms} - 300\text{ ms}$). Standard utility interactions never make the user wait or feel laggy.  Living Superintelligence: AI entities (TEMO, Managers, Workers) never freeze into complete static dead states. They exhibit organic, low-frequency breathing cycles ($0.2\text{ Hz}$ sine wave), micro-saccadic focal adjustments, and active core rotations at all times.  Hierarchy of Motion Scale: Normal UI elements (buttons, inputs, cards) use subtle micro-interactions. High-priority AI orchestrations (Mission Start, Manager Activation, Boot Sequence) receive sweeping, cinematic, spatial transitions.  Spatial Continuity: Cards, modals, and avatars exist in a persistent 3D glass environment. Motion respects Z-axis depth—elements do not simply jump into view; they glide, scale, and defocus along linear perspective corridors.  2. Global Animation LanguageAll motion across the OS utilizes standard physics categories:Utility Motion: Used for standard controls, navigation rails, and inputs. Linear or smooth cubic-bezier transitions designed for speed and clarity.  Spatial Motion: Used for floating glass cards, modals, and camera repositioning in Zone A–E. Incorporates slight scale changes ($0.95\times \leftrightarrow 1.05\times$) combined with Z-axis offsets.  Kinetic Plasma / Energy Motion: Used for AI character cores, voice visualizers, particle conduits, and mission progress vectors. Continuous $60\text{ fps}$ GPU-driven particle simulations and emissive light sweeps.  3.
