## 1. Project Vision

The visual identity of TEMO AI OS establishes an ultra-premium AI Operations Center and character ecosystem[cite: 2, 3]. It bridges the aesthetic philosophy of Apple's minimalist luxury, Iron Man’s holographic laboratory, and a futuristic NASA Mission Control[cite: 2, 3]. Rendered with AAA game engine precision (Unreal Engine 5, Octane Render), the design avoids aggressive cyberpunk clutter, industrial decay, military armor, or distressed textures[cite: 1, 2, 3]. Every element—from architecture to humanoid AI managers—embodies superintelligence, high-key precision, clean architectural glassmorphism, and sophisticated executive authority[cite: 2, 3].

## 7. Motion System

### Bipedal & Core Idle Frequencies
* **Breathing Cycle:** Bipedal posture breathing cycles at $0.2\text{ Hz}$ ($5\text{ s}$ sinusoidal period)[cite: 1, 3].
* **Core Rotation:** Inner energy core rings rotate clockwise continuously ($3\text{ s}$ loop) with ambient cyan pulse[cite: 3].
* **Floating Bobbing:** All suspended UI panels execute a gentle vertical sine-wave motion ($\pm 4\text{ px}$ to $\pm 6\text{ px}$ offset, $4\text{ s} - 6\text{ s}$ duration) with staggered phase delays ($1.2\text{ s}$ offsets between characters)[cite: 1, 3].

### Camera Movement & Composition
* **Default View (Panorama):** $24\text{ mm}$ ultra-wide lens at $1.65\text{ m}$ eye level, centered on the stage pedestal in 1-point perspective convergence[cite: 2].
* **Character Focus View:** $50\text{ mm} - 85\text{ mm}$ portrait focal length, $f/2.8$ depth of field focused on the character's chest core[cite: 3]. Background glass is blurred[cite: 3].
* **Analytics View:** $35\text{ mm}$ focal length angled $30^\circ$ left to frame Zone A & B displays[cite: 2].

### Transitions & Dynamic Behaviors
* **Servo Posture Transitions:** All character posture shifts use an ease-in-out cubic curve over $0.6\text{ s}$[cite: 1].
* **HUD Expansion:** Active interaction triggers a $1.05\times$ scale expansion of the card while sliding $10\text{ px}$ along the Z-axis toward the camera[cite: 3].
* **Startup Sequence:** Ceiling and floor rings pulse sequentially outward from center; glass panels fade up from $0\%$ to $35\%$ opacity with a sweeping light band[cite: 2].

## 8. Materials & Rendering

### Optical & Surface Specifications
* **Viewport Glass:** High-transparency smart glass with minimal chromatic aberration, refractive index (IOR) of $1.52$[cite: 2, 3].
* **Obsidian Floor Glass:** Specularity $0.85$, roughness $0.05$ for sharp, distortion-free mirror reflections[cite: 2].
* **Brushed Titanium Metal:** Anisotropic directional brush effect, medium roughness ($0.3$), high metalness ($0.9$)[cite: 2, 3].
* **Synthetic Bio-Dermis:** Subsurface scattering (SSS) enabled for porcelain white skin elements, giving subtle depth under direct volumetric light[cite: 3].
* **Emissive Surfaces:** Core energy lights and floor neon rings rendered with Bloom and Ray-Traced Global Illumination (GI) bounce[cite: 2, 3].
* **Volumetrics:** $45^\circ$ directional god-rays ($15\%$ opacity cyan) and ground-plane fog layer[cite: 2, 3].

## 10. Things That Must Never Change

1. **No Cyberpunk Clutter:** Strictly forbid neon graffiti, exposed cables, hydraulic fluid, rust, or distressed textures[cite: 1, 3].
2. **No Military Aesthetics:** Absolutely no armor plating, weapons, shoulder spikes, or aggressive combat poses[cite: 1, 3].
3. **No Organic Hair or Helmets:** Characters have bald synthetic domes with visible structural panel seams[cite: 3].
4. **Primary Color Integrity:** Base environment and TEMO use Cyan (`#00F3FF`) and Electric Blue (`#0088FF`)[cite: 3]. Warm colors (reds, amber) are reserved exclusively for manager themes or critical system alert states[cite: 1, 2, 3].
5. **Center Stage Openness:** The central circular pedestal (Zone C) and spatial buffer (Zone D) must remain uncluttered to ensure clear sightlines for character avatars and UI overlays[cite: 2, 3].
6. **Uniform Material Palette:** All assets must strictly utilize the four core materials: Polished White Titanium, Smoked Curved Glass, Matte Carbon-Polymer, and Synthetic Bio-Dermis[cite: 1, 3].
