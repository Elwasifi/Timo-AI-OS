## 2. System-Wide Design Rules & Guidelines

---

### A. Shared Design Language

* **Base Chassis:** All managers share a unified white titanium synthetic body structure (`#F8FAFC` base) with identical articulation joint systems at wrists, elbows, knees, and spine.
* **Core Feature:** Every manager features a central chest reactor core housing their department’s signature energy color.
* **Executive Aesthetic:** Clothing elements combine bespoke human executive tailoring (blazers, waistlines, tunics) with bio-mechanical suit integrations.
* **Material Matrix:** Strict adherence to 4 core materials: Polished White Titanium, Smoked Curved Glass, Matte Black Carbon-Polymer, and Sub-Dermal Bioluminescent Fiber.

### B. Consistency Rules

1. **Height & Scale Uniformity:** Male chassis models stand at $1.88\text{ m}$; female chassis models stand at $1.78\text{ m}$. Scale must remain identical when framed side-by-side.
2. **No Industrial Clutter:** Absolutely no exposed wires, hydraulic fluid leaks, battle scuffs, rust, or militarized armor plates.
3. **Unified Face Language:** All facial topologies are synthetic-porcelain smooth, avoiding exaggerated hyper-expressive human features while maintaining professional warmth.

### C. Animation Rules

* **Idle Frequency:** All bipedal idle breathing animations cycle at $0.2\text{ Hz}$ ($5\text{ s}$ sinusoidal period).
* **UI Floating Offset:** Holographic elements floating near managers bob vertically by $\pm 4\text{ px}$ with out-of-phase staggered delays ($1.2\text{ s}$ offsets between characters).
* **Transitions:** All posture changes use an ease-in-out cubic curve over $0.6\text{ s}$ to simulate precision servo motor control.

### D. Lighting Rules

* **Primary Environment Lighting:** $6500\text{ K}$ cold ambient fill coming from the ceiling and glass floor panels.
* **Character Rim Lights:** Each manager receives an edge rim light matching their departmental color theme at $25\%$ intensity.
* **Core Illumination:** Chest cores emit a point light source ($1.5\text{ m}$ radius) that casts colored volumetric soft shadows onto their lapels and jawlines.
